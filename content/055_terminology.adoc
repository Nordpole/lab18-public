|LLM embedding
|In the context of large language models (LLMs), an embedding is a dense, fixed-length vector representation of text (words, sentences, paragraphs, or even documents) that captures the semantic meaning of that text in a high-dimensional space.

|LLM
|An LLM (Large Language Model) is an advanced artificial intelligence system trained on vast amounts of text data to understand and generate human language.
At its core, an LLM exists primarily as a massive collection of weight parameters organized in a neural network architecture.

|LLM MCP
|In the context of Large Language Models (LLMs), MCP usually stands for Model Control Plane. It’s an architectural layer or service that centralizes the management, governance, and operationalization of machine-learning and LLM assets across an organization.


|Upstream
|Refers toward source-code origin end of open source software development process and related open source community collaboration, documentation, and testing.

|Downstream
|Refers toward “productized” deployable, supported artifact (signed binary) end of open source software development process and related contracted errata, event-based support artifacts, product engineering, and product-specific documentation.
